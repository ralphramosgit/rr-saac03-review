# AWS Machine Learning Services - SAA-C03 Study Note

For the **AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate (SAA-C03)** exam, you generally do not need to know how to _code_ these services. Instead, focus on **identifying the right service for a specific business problem**.

Key Concept: Most of these are "Serverless" and "Managed Services" (High Availability is built-in).

## 1. Amazon Rekognition

**"Computer Vision / Image & Video Analysis"**

- **What it does:** Uses ML to analyze images and videos. You send it an image; it tells you what is inside.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Object & Scene Detection:** Identifies cars, pets, beaches, cities.
    - **Facial Analysis:** Detects faces, gender, age range, emotions (happy/sad).
    - **Facial Recognition:** Matches a face against a private collection (e.g., employee badge entry).
    - **Content Moderation:** Automatically detects unsafe, offensive, or inappropriate content (nudity, violence).
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company needs to automatically flag inappropriate images uploaded by users to a social media app." -> **Use Rekognition Content Moderation.**
    - "A store wants to track the path of customers walking through aisles." -> **Use Rekognition Video Pathing.**

## 2. Amazon Transcribe

**"Speech-to-Text"**

- **What it does:** Converts audio (MP3, WAV, etc.) into text.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Speaker Identification:** Can tell "Speaker A" from "Speaker B".
    - **Automatic Language Identification:** Detects the dominant language.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company needs to generate subtitles/captions for their video content automatically."    -> **Use Transcribe.**
    - "You need to create searchable text transcripts from customer service call recordings." -> **Use Transcribe.**

## 3. Amazon Polly

**"Text-to-Speech"**

- **What it does:** Converts text into lifelike speech (MP3). It is the opposite of Transcribe.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Neural TTS:** Higher quality, more natural sounding voices.
    - **Lexicons:** Customize pronunciation of specific words (e.g., "AWS" as "A-W-S" instead of "Aws").
    - **SSML (Speech Synthesis Markup Language):** Markup tags to control breathing, volume, speed, and emphasis.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "An app needs to read articles aloud to visually impaired users." -> **Use Polly.**

## 4. Amazon Translate

**"Language Translation"**

- **What it does:** Provides high-quality neural machine translation between languages.
- **Key Features:**
    - Real-time and Batch translation.
    - Supports custom terminology (e.g., ensuring brand names aren't translated literally).
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company wants to localize their website content into 10 different languages dynamically." -> **Use Translate.**

## 5. Amazon Lex + Amazon Connect

**"Chatbots & Call Centers"**

### Amazon Lex (The "Brain")

- **What it does:** Provides Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Natural Language Understanding (NLU). It is the technology behind **Alexa**.
- **Use Case:** Building conversational interfaces (Chatbots) for text or voice.

### Amazon Connect (The "Phone System")

- **What it does:** A cloud-based **Contact Center** as a Service.
- **Integration:** You use **Connect** to handle the phone lines and routing, and you use **Lex** to build the automated "IVR" (Interactive Voice Response) that talks to the customer before an agent picks up.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company needs a cloud-based call center that can handle spikes in traffic and uses AI to answer common questions." -> **Use Amazon Connect integrated with Amazon Lex.**

## 6. Amazon Comprehend

**"Natural Language Processing (NLP)"**

- **What it does:** Analyzes text to understand the _meaning_ and _relationships_ within it.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Sentiment Analysis:** Determines if text is Positive, Negative, Neutral, or Mixed.
    - **Entity Recognition:** Extracts people, places, brands, dates.
    - **PII Detection:** Detects Personally Identifiable Information (emails, SSNs) for redaction.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company wants to analyze customer emails to determine if they are happy or angry." -> **Use Comprehend Sentiment Analysis.**

## 7. Amazon Comprehend Medical

**"NLP for Healthcare"**

- **What it does:** Specifically trained on medical text to identify medical conditions, medications, dosages, and Protected Health Information (PHI).
- **Key Features:**
    - **HIPAA Eligible.**
    - Detects PHI (Patient Health Information) for anonymization.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A hospital needs to index doctor's notes and identify patient diagnosis and prescribed medication." -> **Use Comprehend Medical.** (Do not choose standard Comprehend).

## 8. Amazon SageMaker

**"ML for Developers / Custom Models"**

- **What it does:** A fully managed service to build, train, and deploy **custom** machine learning models.
- **Key Concept:** Used when the "ready-made" AI services (like Rekognition or Polly) are not enough and you need to build your own algorithm.
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A data science team needs a managed environment to run Jupyter Notebooks and train TensorFlow models." -> **Use SageMaker.**
    - "You need to deploy a custom fraud detection model with auto-scaling." -> **Use SageMaker.**

## 9. Amazon Kendra

**"Enterprise Search"**

- **What it does:** An intelligent document search service powered by ML. Think of it as "Google Search" for your internal company documents.
- **Key Features:**
    - Extracts answers from PDFs, Word docs, HTML, SharePoint, etc.
    - Supports **Natural Language Queries** (e.g., "Where is the IT support desk?" vs. matching keyword "IT").
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company wants to allow employees to search through an internal repository of PDF manuals using natural language questions." -> **Use Kendra.**

## 10. Amazon Personalize

**"Real-time Recommendations"**

- **What it does:** Machine learning service for creating real-time personalized recommendations. Same technology used by Amazon.com for "Items you might like".
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "An e-commerce website wants to show a 'Recommended for you' section based on user purchase history." -> **Use Personalize.** (Do not build a custom model in SageMaker if Personalize can do it).

## 11. Amazon Textract

**"OCR on Steroids / Document Analysis"**

- **What it does:** Extracts text, handwriting, and **data** from scanned documents.
- **Key Difference vs. OCR:** Standard OCR just gives you a blob of text. Textract understands **Structure** (Tables, Forms, Key-Value pairs).
- **Exam Scenario:**
    - "A company needs to extract total amounts and vendor names from thousands of scanned PDF invoices and load them into a database." -> **Use Textract.**

|Service|Keyword / Trigger|
|---|---|
|**Rekognition**|Images, Video, Faces, Content Moderation (Safety), Labels.|
|**Transcribe**|Speech-to-Text, Subtitles, Meeting minutes, Audio files to text.|
|**Polly**|Text-to-Speech, Talking app, SSML, Voices.|
|**Translate**|Localization, Language Translation.|
|**Lex**|Chatbots, Conversational AI, Alexa tech.|
|**Connect**|Cloud Contact Center, Call Center, Phone system.|
|**Comprehend**|Sentiment Analysis, NLP, Entities, PII redaction.|
|**SageMaker**|Custom Models, Jupyter Notebooks, Build/Train/Deploy own algorithm.|
|**Kendra**|Internal Search, Document Search, Natural Language Queries.|
|**Personalize**|Recommendations, "Users who bought this also bought...", Retail ML.|
|**Textract**|Scanned Documents, Forms, Tables, Invoices, Receipts, OCR.|
